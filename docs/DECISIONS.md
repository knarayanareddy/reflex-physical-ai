A decision is recorded when it is made, not when it is finished. If a ticket is
parked or killed, it is recorded here with the reason.

| 2026-10-26 | The **first** stopping rule in priority order names the outcome; a clamp is recorded but cannot outrank a veto | A refusal must have one attributable cause. | 2026-10-26 | A rule that raises becomes a **VETO**, and an unimplemented taxonomy rule is a VETO | The alternative — skipping the rule — makes the envelope silently optional on the one day a detector bug appears | Propagating the exception (rejected: the caller would have to decide safety, which is the envelope's job) | Lead |
