# Submission Checklist & Disqualifiers

**Deadline: Friday 30 October 2026, 10:00 am PT = 17:00 UTC = 19:00 CEST.**
Target: **submit by Thursday 29 October, 18:00 CEST** — 25 hours of buffer. Devpost traffic spikes at the
deadline and a saved draft is *not* a final submission.

---

## A. Stage-1 pass/fail filters (fail any one → never scored)

- [ ] Project **runs on Nebius Token Factory or Nebius AI Cloud** at *runtime* — not merely "we tested it there"
- [ ] Project uses **≥1 NVIDIA open-source model** — exact model IDs named in the README
- [ ] Project **reasonably fits the declared track**
- [ ] The required APIs/SDKs are **genuinely applied** — the rules state a *"superficial rebrand of an unrelated idea is not sufficient"*
- [ ] Submitted **during the submission period** (26 Aug – 30 Oct 2026) and **finalised**, not left as a draft
- [ ] Eligibility: above the age of majority in your country of residence; not in an excluded
      country/territory (rules list Brazil, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Quebec, Russia among the exclusions)
- [ ] If the project **existed before 26 Aug 2026**: a written explanation of **what significantly changed during
      the submission period** is included

## B. Required artifacts

- [ ] **Working project** built with NVIDIA Nemotron (or another NVIDIA open-source model) on Token Factory or AI Cloud, meeting track requirements
- [ ] **Category / track** selected — the one that genuinely fits best
- [ ] **Project description** — what you created, **why** you created it, and **how it works**
- [ ] **Working demo** — URL to a working demo, hosted application, or test build
      *(**not required for Physical AI** submissions)*
- [ ] **Demo video** — **public YouTube**, **3 minutes or shorter**. Judges *"need not watch beyond three minutes"* → target **2:50**
      - Shows the project working
      - Shows **how you used Nebius Token Factory** and **NVIDIA Nemotron or other NVIDIA open-source models**
      - **Physical AI only:** must include **≥1 minute** of footage of the physical hardware/robot actually
        operating — or, if there is no hardware component, of the key application modules in action
      - **No third-party trademarks, copyrighted music, or other protected material without permission**
- [ ] **Public code repository** (GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket), submitted as a URL for judging and testing
      - **An open-source licence (Apache 2.0, MIT or MPL 2.0) visible at the TOP of the repository page** — i.e. a
        `LICENSE` file in the repo root, not buried in `/docs`
      - **README with setup instructions and clear guidance for running the project**
      - README **highlights**: how you used NVIDIA Nemotron / other NVIDIA open-source models, **where Token
        Factory accelerated your workflow**, and **any other Nebius tools or services used**
- [ ] **Feedback** on Nebius Token Factory, AI Cloud, and any NVIDIA tools/models/technologies used
      → this is the **Most Valuable Feedback** prize: 10 × $100 + NVIDIA swag, judged on *completeness, viability
      and potential impact*. ~20 minutes of work. Do not skip it.
- [ ] **City Winner Awards** ($500 × 20): only if you attended a Builders & Brews event — select your city.
      (Published cities: Tokyo, Da Nang, Seoul, Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, Taipei, Tel Aviv, London, Copenhagen,
      Stockholm, Warsaw, Amsterdam, Berlin, Paris, Mexico City, NYC, Toronto, Boston, SF, LA. **No Dutch city
      other than Amsterdam**, and attendance was required.)

## C. Durability requirements (people lose here in December)

- [ ] The project **remains available to judges free of charge and without restriction until the judging period
      ends** (judging runs **1–15 Dec 2026**, winners ~**11 Jan 2027**)
- [ ] Demo URL does not require a login, an invite code, a credit card or a paid tier
- [ ] Demo hosting free tier will not expire or sleep before mid-December — check the plan's limits
- [ ] Any API keys the demo needs are **yours** and will stay funded; do not let credits lapse
- [ ] Video stays **public** (not unlisted-then-privated, not age-restricted)
- [ ] Repo stays **public** and the `LICENSE` stays at the root
- [ ] The README's run instructions work from a **clean clone on a clean machine** — test this on 29 Oct
- [ ] If your backend is a Serverless Endpoint that stops when idle, document how a judge restarts it, **or keep it warm**

## D. Prize-mechanics reminders

- [ ] **Award stacking:** *"one (1) Overall Award OR one (1) Track Award and one (1) Bonus Award."* Plan for one path.
- [ ] **Best Use of Tavily ($3,000)** requires *"a functional runtime call to the Tavily API as part of the solution"* — log the calls and show them in the UI/video
- [ ] Winner administration: identity/eligibility/contribution verification; **required winner forms must be
      returned within 10 business days** of being sent; prizes delivered within **60 days** of completed forms;
      team prizes are paid to the team Representative who allocates them
- [ ] Keep every team member's real contribution documented (commits, ticket reports) — winners are subject to
      contribution/role verification

## E. Final 60 minutes before you press submit

1. Open the Devpost submission form in one tab, your repo in another, the demo in a third (**fresh incognito
   window, different machine or a phone on cellular**), and the YouTube video in a fourth.
2. Confirm the video is **public** and **≤3:00**.
3. Confirm the repo shows the **licence badge at the top of the page**.
4. Clone the repo to a temp dir. Follow the README verbatim. Does it run?
5. Click the demo. Does it reach the aha in under two minutes **with no one explaining it**?
6. Paste the demo URL into your phone's browser. Does the layout survive?
7. Read your project description aloud. Is the **one-sentence problem** the first thing? Is the **insider
   insight** stated? Is there a **real number from a real authority**?
8. Check the **feedback** field is filled with specific, technical, constructive content.
9. Press submit. Screenshot the confirmation. Email it to every teammate.
